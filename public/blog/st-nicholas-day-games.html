St Nicholas Day - games and ideas for 6 December

🎅

In Poland, Germany, Austria and other parts of Europe, children celebrate St Nicholas Day on 6 December. The evening before, they clean their boots and leave them out, and in the morning they find sweets and small gifts inside. If your family keeps this tradition - at home or abroad - here are games that fit the day: on your phone, free and with no sign-up.

🎅 At home🏫 At school🎁 At work

💬 My St Nicholas Day in Poland

In Poland the day is called Mikołajki. I remember how, as a little girl, I had to learn a poem by heart and recite it in front of a crowd of people at my mum's workplace. The stress was HUGE! But it was worth it - as a reward I got a parcel full of delicious sweets, which were a real treat back then. I still remember how they tasted!

Now I'm a mum myself, and I'm the one putting together small, symbolic parcels for my kids. I hope that one day they'll look back on these moments with the same smile as I do.

And in the evening? A family round of Charades! 🎭 In our favourite category, “Animals”, of course, and in the quick version - so this special day ends as happily as it began. 🥰

- Aga, creator of these games

🏫 At school and nursery - no phones needed

Younger children usually don't have phones, and many schools don't allow them. For a lesson or a class party, paper, a whiteboard and our word list are enough:

🤫 Charades in class - the teacher whispers a word from the list below to one child, the child acts it out and the class guesses. Play as a whole class or in two teams.

🪢 Hangman on the board - for older children who can already read and write. The teacher draws dashes for the letters and the children guess one letter at a time.

🔵 Dots and Boxes printable - played in pairs, all you need is paper and a pen. Even nursery children can play.

👉 Printable sheet

🌍 Countries & Cities printable - for classes where children can already write. The teacher picks a letter and the children fill in their answers.
