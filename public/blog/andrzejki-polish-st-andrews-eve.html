Andrzejki: a Polish fortune-telling night and games for the evening

🕯️

Andrzejki falls on 29 November, but most people celebrate it on the last weekend of November. If you have Polish friends or colleagues, you may well get invited. Below you'll find what it's about, the traditional fortune-telling and games that work well afterwards - at home, at work and online.

🇵🇱 What is Andrzejki and how do Polish people celebrate it?

Andrzejki (pronounced roughly "an-JAY-kee") is the evening before St Andrew's Day. In Poland it's a night of fortune-telling. It used to be mostly for young women wondering who they would marry. Today everyone joins in: friends, families, schools and offices hold small parties. It's not a public holiday, just a relaxed evening that people look forward to. In Scotland, 30 November is St Andrew's Day, the national day. In Poland, the night before is about guessing the future.

🕯️ The fortune-telling

  • Pouring wax - melted wax is poured through the hole of a key into cold water. Then you look at the shadow of the hardened shape on the wall and guess what it shows.
  • The shoe race - everyone puts one shoe in a line, heel to toe, towards the door. Whoever's shoe reaches the doorstep first will be the first to marry.
  • The cups - small objects are hidden under upturned cups, for example a ring, a coin and a heart. You pick a cup, and the object under it tells you what's coming.

If you like guessing shapes in the wax, you'll enjoy Sketch & Guess - it's a similar idea, but with drawings.

🎲 When you meet in one place

Everyone plays on their own phone, but you're together.

🤥 2 Truths 1 Lie - everyone writes three sentences about themselves, one made up. It fits the fortune-telling theme: the sentences can be about the future, e.g. "Next year I'll live in a different city." 3-20 players.

👉 Play: 2 Truths 1 Lie

🎨 Sketch & Guess - everyone writes a word, then you take turns drawing and guessing. 3-10 players.

👉 Play: Sketch & Guess

🤫 Charades - act out words without speaking, in teams. 4-40 players.

👉 Play: Charades

❓ Who Am I? - guess who you are by asking yes/no questions. 2-16 players.

❓ Questions

When is Andrzejki?

On the evening of 29 November, the eve of St Andrew's Day. Many people celebrate on the nearest weekend.

Is it only for Polish people?

No. Anyone can join - the fortune-telling and games need no Polish.
