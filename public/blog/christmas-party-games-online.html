Christmas party games for work and family

🎄

At an office Christmas party or after a family dinner, there's often a moment when the chat dies down and everyone reaches for their phone. These games are made for that moment: one person creates a room, the others enter the code and you play - each on your own phone.

🏢 Work party🏠 Family Christmas🎄 20 words

🏢 For the office Christmas party (in person or online)

🎯 Corporate Bingo - for the last meeting before the holidays. The cards are full of the corporate speak you hear in every meeting: "Synergy", "Value add", "Can you hear me?". Tick a phrase off when someone says it. First full line wins. 2-50 players.

👉 Play: Corporate Bingo

🤥 2 Truths 1 Lie - everyone writes three sentences about themselves, one made up. The rest guess which. You'll learn more about your colleagues than in a year of meetings. 3-20 players.

👉 Play: 2 Truths 1 Lie

🎭 Forbidden Words - describe a word to your team without using the five most obvious words. Harder than it sounds. 4-12 players.

👉 Play: Forbidden Words

🤫 Charades - the classic, in teams. The phone shows the word, you act it out without speaking. Great for a big room. 4-40 players.

👉 Play: Charades

🏠 For the family at Christmas

🤫 Charades - kids are great at it, and adults can help the youngest work out how to act something.

👉 Play: Charades

❓ Who Am I? - guess who you are by asking yes/no questions. On the easy level with animals, even preschoolers can play. 2-16 players.

👉 Play: Who Am I?

🧩 Find Pairs - find the matching cards. No reading or writing needed, so the youngest can play too - and they often beat the adults. 2-6 players.

👉 Play: Find Pairs

🎨 Sketch & Guess - everyone writes a word, then you take turns drawing and guessing. A Christmas "snowman" becomes a "potato in a hat" after three rounds. 3-10 players.

👉 Play: Sketch & Guess

🪢 Hangman - one person picks a word, the others guess letters. Use the list below. 2-10 players.

👉 Play: Hangman

And of course Countries & Cities - no paper or pens, and the points add up by themselves. 2-12 players.

🌍 Christmas apart

If you live abroad and your family is in another country (or the other way round), you can still play together. Start a video call on WhatsApp, Messenger or Zoom and open the game in the browser - everyone on their own device, in the same game room. Countries & Cities, Who Am I? and Charades (you act in front of the camera) work best.

💬 How we play at home

My family plays Charades most of all, or Who Am I? on the easy level. When our youngest joins in, we pick animals and one adult helps him ask the questions. In Charades we help each other work out how to act out a character or an animal. We also love Find Pairs - my son loves winning it! He doesn't have his own phone yet and can't write, so we haven't played Sketch & Guess yet. I can't wait until he's old enough for the writing games too.

- Aga, creator of these games

⏱️ How to start (2 minutes)

  1. Pick a game and tap "Create room".
  2. Send the code or link - on WhatsApp, Teams, Messenger.
  3. When everyone has joined, tap "Start game".

Works on phones, tablets and computers. On an online call, keep the call in one window and the game in another.
