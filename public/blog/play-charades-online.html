What is Charades?

One player gets a secret word on their screen and has to act it out silently. No talking, no sounds, no pointing at objects in the room. Their team shouts guesses out loud — the actor taps ✅ on their screen when someone gets it right. The team with the most correct guesses wins.

Simple to explain, impossible to play with a straight face. That's why it works equally well in a living room, a campsite, or a family dinner table.

How it Works

  • Everyone joins the same room from their own phone or laptop
  • Players split into two teams — red and blue
  • Each round, one player sees a secret word and acts it out
  • Their team shouts guesses — the actor taps ✅ when correct
  • If the word is too hard, the actor can skip it
  • Timer runs — if it hits zero, no point for either team
  • Teams take turns until all rounds are played

How teams work: Players are split automatically into two teams when they join. The host can reassign anyone before the game starts. Teams stay balanced — no one has to argue about who goes where.

Word Categories

The host picks a category before the game starts:

  • Animals — great for kids and mixed groups
  • Actions — everyday things that are surprisingly hard to mime
  • Professions — dentist, architect, barman… some are brutal
  • Movie characters — actors, heroes, screen icons. Great for groups who know their films
  • Food — simpler, good for warming up
  • Sports — best with people who actually follow sport
  • Mixed — random pick from all categories, keeps everyone on their toes

Game Length

Three options: Quick (one word per player per team), Standard (two words), or Marathon (three words). Standard is the sweet spot for most groups — long enough to build momentum, short enough that nobody loses interest.

Charades vs Who Am I?

Both need zero equipment and work on any phone. But they're completely different games.

🎬 Charades

  • Two teams compete
  • Actor sees the word, team shouts guesses
  • Loud, physical, chaotic
  • Best with 6–20 players
  • Perfect for energetic groups
VS

❓ Who Am I?

  • No teams — everyone plays individually
  • Everyone sees your character except you
  • Ask yes/no questions to figure out who you are
  • Best with 4–12 players
  • Perfect for ice breakers

Start with Charades — loud, physical, gets any group going. When the energy drops, Who Am I? gives everyone something to think about.
