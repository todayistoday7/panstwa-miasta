What Is Charades?

Charades is a classic guessing game where one player acts out a word or phrase using only body language and gestures — no talking, no pointing at objects, no mouthing words. Their team tries to guess what they're acting out before the timer runs out.

Our online version keeps everything you love about the original game while adding automatic word selection, scoring, team management, and a built-in timer — so you can focus on the fun.

  • A camera or same room — others need to see the actor's gestures
  • No download, no signup — just share the room code and play

How to Set Up a Game

Setting up a game of Charades online takes less than 30 seconds:

  • Create a room — enter your name and hit "Create Room"
  • Share the code — give the 5-letter room code to your friends
  • Organise teams — players join and the host assigns them to Red or Blue team
  • Choose settings — pick word categories, difficulty, and game length
  • Start playing! — the host hits Start when everyone's ready

How to Play — Step by Step

  • Step 1: One player from the active team becomes the actor
  • Step 2: A word appears on the actor's screen — only they can see it
  • Step 3: The actor performs the word using gestures, facial expressions, and body movement
  • Step 4: Their team shouts out guesses — the host confirms when correct
  • Step 5: Teams alternate until all rounds are complete

💡 Pro tip: Use big, exaggerated gestures. Act out the overall concept rather than individual words. If the word is "swimming" — mime the whole action, don't try to spell it out!

Scoring & Game Length

  • Correct guess — the acting team scores a point
  • Time runs out — no points, the other team gets a turn
  • Skip — the actor can skip a difficult word (limited per round)

Choose your game length before starting:

  • Quick (1×) — each player acts once
  • Standard (2×) — each player acts twice
  • Marathon (3×) — each player acts three times

Tips for Acting Out Words

  • Start big — establish the general category with broad gestures
  • Use the "sounds like" gesture — cup your ear if you're acting a rhyming word
  • Show the number of words — hold up fingers to indicate how many words in the phrase
  • Break it down — act out syllables or parts of the word separately
  • React to guesses — nod enthusiastically when teammates are getting close

Rules Recap — The Do's and Don'ts

  • ✅ Use gestures, facial expressions, and body movement
  • ✅ Point at yourself or mime actions
  • ✅ Nod or shake your head to guide your team
  • ❌ No talking, humming, or making sounds
  • ❌ No pointing at objects in the room
  • ❌ No mouthing words or spelling in the air
