How to Play Forbidden Words Online

Describe a secret word without the words you'd most like to say. Free, 2 to 12 players, everyone on their own phone, no sign-up.

2 or 3 players - everyone for themselves

  • No teams - everyone plays for themselves. You take turns being the describer, and before each turn you can see who is next.
  • The card - the describer sees the word on their phone, with a few forbidden words under it. They have 30, 60 or 90 seconds (the host sets it) to get the others to guess as many words as possible.
  • Words only - you can't say the word or any part of it, the forbidden words, act it out with your hands or give "sounds like…" hints.
  • Guessing - the others guess out loud. When someone gets it, the describer taps ✅ and gets the next word straight away. Too hard? Tap Skip - no penalty.
  • Oops - let a forbidden word slip? The describer honestly taps 🙊 - they lose 1 point and the word is gone.
  • Points - each word guessed is 1 point for the describer. After the last turn, the player with the most points wins.

4 or more players - two teams

  • Describer - one player describes to their own team.
  • Referee - a referee from the other team sees the same card. If the describer says a forbidden word, the referee taps the penalty: the other team gets 1 point and the word is gone.
  • Points - guessed word = 1 point. Skip = no penalty.
  • Words only - no gestures, no "sounds like…".

After the last turn, the team with more points wins.

💡 Tip: Say what it's for or when you use it, not what it looks like.

Time and game length

  • Turn time - 30, 60 or 90 seconds per turn.
  • Game length - quick, standard or marathon: everyone describes once, twice or three times.
  • Words - in English, Polish, German or Swedish.
