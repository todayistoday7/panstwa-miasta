How to Play Hangman Online

One player thinks of a secret word, the others guess it letter by letter. Free, 2 to 10 players, everyone on their own phone, no sign-up.

Taking turns

Each round one player is the word picker. They type a secret word (and a hint if they want) or tap Random to get one from the word bank. Everyone else guesses. The picker role moves round the group.

A round

  • No turns - every guesser can tap a letter at any time.
  • Shared mistakes - letters and mistakes are shared: a wrong letter from anyone adds a piece to the hangman.
  • 7 wrong letters - the hangman is complete and the picker wins the round.
  • Guess the whole word - think you know the word? You can type the whole word at any time - right, you win the round; wrong, the round is lost for everyone.

Points

  • 1 point to whoever completes the word - with the last letter or the whole word.
  • 1 point to the picker if nobody gets it.

Game length

Quick, standard or marathon - everyone picks a word once, twice or three times. Then the player with the most points wins.

💡 Tip for the picker: short words with unusual letters are harder than long ones - JAZZ beats ELEPHANT.
